02 / Projects & practice

Making sense.
Making things.

I’m drawn to the space between an ambiguous problem and a system that actually helps someone.

Areas of practice

What I work on.

These are the areas I work in and explore. For now, this is a map of my practice; individual project write-ups will live here as I prepare them.

01

Software / Systems / Processes

Software & information architecture

I write software and reverse-engineer systems. I like finding the structure inside a complicated process, then making it understandable enough to improve.

Questions I bring+

What information needs to move? Who needs it? Where are assumptions hiding? What does the person using this actually need?

02

Manufacturing / Workflow / People

Manufacturing systems

I’m interested in the connection between software and the way work happens on the floor: the constraints, the handoffs, and the gap between a process on paper and a process in use.

What draws me in+

Several constraints at once. People with different kinds of expertise. Problems that become clearer when you trace how the whole system behaves.

03

3D printing / Prototyping / Curiosity

Making & experimentation

3D printing and making things give ideas somewhere to meet reality. I enjoy moving between a model of how something should work and the details of making it work.

How I approach it+

Start with the question. Try something concrete. Pay attention to the result, especially when it disagrees with the model.

Let’s think it through

Have a problem to untangle?

Bring the constraints, the confusing part, or the explanation that never quite made sense. That’s often a good place to start.
